About Us

Building an inclusive community for Ambedkarite Buddhists in Canada

Our Mission

The Ambedkarite Buddhist Community Canada is dedicated to creating a supportive and inclusive environment for Ambedkarite Buddhists across Canada. We strive to promote the principles of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar – equality, education, and social justice – while preserving and sharing Buddhist teachings.

We provide comprehensive support to community members, especially students arriving in Canada, through mentorship programs, cultural events, and educational resources.

Our Vision

To establish a thriving network of Ambedkarite Buddhist communities across Canada, united by shared values and commitment to social progress. We envision:

  • A permanent Vihara serving as our cultural and spiritual hub
  • Comprehensive support systems for students and new immigrants
  • Regular educational programs and cultural celebrations
  • Strong partnerships with educational institutions
  • A self-sustaining community organization

Our Story

Founded in 2018, the Ambedkarite Buddhist Community Canada began as a small gathering of like-minded individuals who shared a common heritage and commitment to Dr. Ambedkar's principles. What started with just 15 members in Toronto has grown into a vibrant network of over 500 community members across major Canadian cities.

Our community has been instrumental in helping hundreds of Indian students transition to life in Canada, providing essential support with housing, employment, and cultural integration. We organize regular events celebrating Buddhist festivals, Dr. Ambedkar's legacy, and promote social awareness programs.

Today, we continue to grow stronger, working toward our goal of establishing a permanent Vihara that will serve as a beacon of community support, education, and spiritual growth for generations to come.
