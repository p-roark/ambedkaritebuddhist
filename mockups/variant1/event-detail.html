Buddha Purnima Celebration 2025

📅
May 15, 2025 Thursday
🕒
10:00 AM - 5:00 PM EST
📍
Toronto Community Center 123 Main Street, Toronto, ON

About This Event

Join us for a grand celebration of Buddha Purnima, the most sacred day commemorating the birth, enlightenment, and passing of Lord Buddha. This year's celebration will bring together our community for a day of spirituality, reflection, and fellowship.

Event Schedule

  • 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM: Welcome and traditional lamp lighting ceremony
  • 11:00 AM - 12:30 PM: Buddhist prayers and chanting
  • 12:30 PM - 1:30 PM: Community lunch (vegetarian)
  • 1:30 PM - 2:30 PM: Dharma talk on Buddha's teachings
  • 2:30 PM - 3:30 PM: Guided meditation session
  • 3:30 PM - 4:30 PM: Cultural performances and bhajans
  • 4:30 PM - 5:00 PM: Closing prayers and community networking

What to Bring

  • Meditation cushion or mat (optional)
  • White clothing preferred (traditional)
  • Flowers or offerings for Buddha altar
  • Open mind and peaceful heart

Important Information

  • Event is free for all community members and guests
  • Parking available on-site
  • Wheelchair accessible venue
  • Children are welcome
  • Please register to help us plan for food and seating
