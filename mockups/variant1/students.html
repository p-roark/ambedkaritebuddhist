Essential Information for New Students

Everything you need to know before and after arriving in Canada

📋 Pre-Arrival Checklist

  • Valid study permit and visa
  • Acceptance letter from institution
  • Proof of funds ($20,000+ CAD)
  • Health insurance coverage
  • Temporary accommodation booked
  • Flight tickets and travel documents
  • International driving permit (if needed)
  • Academic transcripts and certificates

🛂 Immigration & Documents

Important documents and procedures:

  • Study Permit: Required for studies > 6 months
  • Work Permit: 20 hrs/week during semester
  • SIN Number: Apply immediately upon arrival
  • Health Card: Province-specific, 3-month wait
  • IRCC Portal: Track your application status
IRCC Website

🏠 Housing Options

On-Campus

Residence halls, convenient but limited availability. Apply early!

Off-Campus

Shared apartments, basement apartments, or private rooms. More affordable.

Useful Websites:

  • Facebook housing groups
  • Kijiji.ca
  • Rentals.ca
  • PadMapper

💡 Community Tip: Connect with our members for housing recommendations and roommate matching!

💳 Banking & Finance

Opening a Bank Account:

  • Passport and study permit required
  • Student accounts with no monthly fees
  • Get a debit card and credit card
  • Set up online banking immediately

Major Banks:

  • TD Bank - Student packages
  • RBC - International student accounts
  • Scotiabank - StartRight Program
  • CIBC - Student banking
  • BMO - Student accounts

⚠️ Important: Build your credit history early! Get a secured credit card if needed.

📱 Phone & Internet

Mobile Plans

  • Rogers, Bell, Telus: Premium carriers
  • Fido, Virgin, Koodo: Mid-tier, better value
  • Public Mobile, Chatr: Budget-friendly

Internet Providers

  • Rogers, Bell (major providers)
  • TekSavvy, Start.ca (affordable options)
  • Most apartments include utilities

🏥 Healthcare

Provincial Health Insurance:

  • OHIP (Ontario), MSP (BC), AHS (Alberta)
  • Usually 3-month waiting period
  • Apply immediately upon arrival

Private Insurance:

  • Required during waiting period
  • University health plans available
  • Guard.me, Blue Cross popular options

Finding Doctors:

  • Walk-in clinics for immediate care
  • Register with family doctor
  • University health centers

Resources for Current Students

Career development, networking, and community support

💼 Career Development

Resume & Cover Letter

  • Use Canadian format (1-2 pages)
  • No photo, age, or marital status
  • Tailor for each application
  • Free templates available online

Job Search Platforms

  • LinkedIn (networking essential!)
  • Indeed.ca
  • Workopolis
  • University career portals
  • Company websites directly
Career Workshops

🎯 Co-op & Internships

Gaining Canadian work experience is crucial for career success.

  • Co-op Programs: Integrated with degree
  • Summer Internships: 4-16 week placements
  • Part-time Jobs: 20 hrs/week during study
  • Volunteer Work: Build experience & network

💡 Tip: Start applying 3-4 months before intended start date. Competition is high!

Mentorship Program

Connect with experienced community members

Looking for a Mentor?

Our mentorship program connects new students with established community members who can provide guidance, support, and advice.

What You Get:

  • One-on-one guidance from experienced professional
  • Career advice and networking opportunities
  • Cultural adjustment support
  • Academic guidance
  • Regular check-ins and meetings
  • Access to mentor's professional network

Become a Mentor

Share your experience and make a meaningful impact on a student's journey in Canada.

Requirements:

  • 3+ years living/working in Canada
  • Commitment of 2-3 hours per month
  • Willingness to share knowledge and experiences
  • Professional or educational background to share

Benefits:

  • Give back to the community
  • Develop leadership skills
  • Expand your network
  • Recognition as community leader
