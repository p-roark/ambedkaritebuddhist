Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a referral link?

You can get a referral link from any existing member of our community. We encourage attending our events to meet current members.

How long does the approval process take?

Applications are typically reviewed within 5-7 business days. You'll receive an email notification once your application is approved.

Is membership really free?

Yes! Membership is completely free. We believe in building an inclusive community focused on shared values rather than financial contributions.

How many people can I refer?

Each member can have up to 5 active referral links at a time. New links can be generated as previous ones are used or expire.
