Membership FAQs

How does the referral system work?

You need a referral link or code from an existing member to apply. This helps us maintain a connected community and ensures new members have a community contact.

How long does approval take?

Most applications are reviewed within 2-3 business days. You'll receive an email notification once your application has been approved.

What if I don't have a referral link?

Attend one of our community events to meet existing members, or contact us directly and we'll help connect you with someone from the community.

What if I move to a different city?

Your membership is valid across all our chapters in Canada. Simply update your address in the member portal, and you can participate in activities at any location.
