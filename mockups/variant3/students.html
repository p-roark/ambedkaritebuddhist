Essential First Steps

1

Apply for SIN (Social Insurance Number)

Required for working and accessing government services. Apply at Service Canada within days of arrival.

2

Open a Canadian Bank Account

Most banks offer special student accounts with no fees. Bring your study permit and passport.

3

Get Health Insurance

Apply for provincial health coverage. Some provinces have a waiting period, so get private insurance to cover the gap.

4

Setup Phone & Internet

Compare phone plans (Rogers, Bell, Telus, Fido). Student discounts are often available.

5

Get Public Transit Pass

Monthly student passes offer significant savings. Available at subway stations and online.

6

Connect with Community

Join our student meetups, find a mentor, and build your support network right away.
