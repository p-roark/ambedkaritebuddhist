About This Event

Join us for a magnificent celebration of Buddha Jayanti, commemorating the birth of Lord Buddha. This special day brings our entire community together for prayer, reflection, and celebration.

The celebration will feature traditional Buddhist prayers and chanting, guided meditation sessions, Dhamma talks by renowned speakers, cultural performances showcasing our rich heritage, and a communal vegetarian feast.

This event is perfect for families, individuals, and anyone interested in learning about Buddhist traditions. All are welcome regardless of their background or level of familiarity with Buddhism.
