About Our Community

Guided by the principles of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, we build bridges, support growth, and celebrate our Buddhist heritage.

🎯

Our Mission

To create an inclusive and supportive community for Ambedkarite Buddhists in Canada, providing resources, education, and opportunities for spiritual and personal growth while preserving our cultural heritage.

👁️

Our Vision

A thriving Ambedkarite Buddhist community across Canada where every member finds support, connection, and opportunities to practice and share the teachings of Buddha and Dr. Ambedkar.

Our Journey

How We Started

Founded in 2015, the Ambedkarite Buddhist Community Canada began as a small gathering of individuals who shared a common vision: to create a space where followers of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar's teachings could come together, support one another, and celebrate their Buddhist faith.

What started with just 20 members in Toronto has grown into a vibrant network of over 500 active members across 10 Canadian cities. We've organized over 150 events, supported 75+ students, and built a strong foundation for future generations.

Today, we continue to honor Dr. Ambedkar's legacy by promoting education, equality, and social justice while providing practical support to community members, especially students and newcomers to Canada.
