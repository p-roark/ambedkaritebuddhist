About Our Sangha

Preserving traditions, building community, transforming lives

🎯 Our Mission

To create a vibrant, inclusive Ambedkarite Buddhist community in Canada that provides spiritual guidance, cultural preservation, and practical support to its members while promoting the transformative teachings of Buddha and the social justice vision of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar.

🔮 Our Vision

A future where every Ambedkarite Buddhist in Canada has access to a supportive community, spiritual resources, and opportunities for growth—where dharma practice and social action unite to create lasting positive change in our lives and society.

📖 Our Story 📖

Founded in 2018, the Ambedkarite Buddhist Community Canada emerged from the growing need for a dedicated space where followers of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar's vision could come together, practice Buddhism, and support one another in a new land.

What began as informal gatherings of a dozen families in Toronto has grown into a vibrant community of over 500 members across Canada. We've organized countless events, supported hundreds of students, and created a home away from home for Ambedkarite Buddhists.

Today, we continue Dr. Ambedkar's legacy by combining spiritual practice with social action, providing both dharma teachings and practical support to create a compassionate, just community.
