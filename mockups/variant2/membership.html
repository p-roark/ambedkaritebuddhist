❓ Membership FAQs ❓

Is membership free?

Yes! Membership is completely free. We grow through a referral-based system where existing members can invite others to join.

How do I get a referral link?

Connect with an existing member at our events or through social media. Members can generate referral links to invite others.

How long does approval take?

Applications are typically reviewed within 3-5 business days. You'll receive an email notification once your application is approved.

Can I support the community financially?

While membership is free, we welcome donations to support our community goals and events. Visit our donations page to contribute.
