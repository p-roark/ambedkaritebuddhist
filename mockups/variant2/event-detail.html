About This Event

Join us for the most sacred day in the Buddhist calendar - Buddha Jayanti (also known as Vesak). This auspicious day commemorates three major events in Buddha's life: his birth, enlightenment, and parinirvana (passing into nirvana).

Our celebration will bring together the entire Ambedkarite Buddhist community for a day of spiritual practice, cultural performances, educational activities, and community bonding. This is an excellent opportunity to deepen your understanding of Buddhist teachings and connect with fellow practitioners.
