Student Support Hub

Comprehensive resources for your Canadian journey

Welcome to Canada! 🇨🇦

We understand the challenges of moving to a new country. Our community is here to support you every step of the way - from your first day in Canada to building a successful career.

Moving to Canada Current Students

🛬 Pre-Arrival Checklist 🛬

📄 Essential Documents

  • ✓ Valid passport and study permit
  • ✓ Letter of acceptance from university
  • ✓ Proof of funds (bank statements)
  • ✓ Academic transcripts and certificates
  • ✓ Medical examination records
  • ✓ Travel insurance documents

💰 Financial Planning

  • ✓ Budget $1,500-2,000 CAD per month
  • ✓ Open international student bank account
  • ✓ Tuition: $15,000-30,000 per year
  • ✓ Rent: $600-1,200 per month (shared)
  • ✓ Food: $300-500 per month
  • ✓ Transportation: $100-150 per month

🏠 Finding Accommodation

Options

  • On-Campus Housing: Convenient but limited availability
  • Shared Apartments: Most affordable option for students
  • Homestay: Live with a Canadian family
  • Private Rentals: More expensive but independent

Useful Websites

  • • Kijiji.ca - Classifieds
  • • Facebook Marketplace - Student groups
  • • ViewIt.ca - Rental listings
  • • PadMapper - Apartment search
Community Help: Contact us for connections with current students who can help you find roommates!

🏦 Banking

  • • Open account before arrival
  • • Major banks: RBC, TD, Scotiabank
  • • Student accounts available
  • • No monthly fees for students

📱 Phone & Internet

  • • Providers: Rogers, Bell, Telus
  • • Budget carriers: Fido, Koodo
  • • Plans from $40-60/month
  • • Buy SIM at airport

🚇 Transportation

  • • TTC (Toronto), TransLink (Vancouver)
  • • Student discounts available
  • • Monthly passes: $100-150
  • • Presto card for Toronto
